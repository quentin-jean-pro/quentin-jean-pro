## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Software & AI Engineer passionate about **bridging physical engineering and intelligent cognitive architectures**. My focus is building robust, production-grade AI systems that solve complex industrial challenges through:
- **Agentic Architectures & MCP:** Designing distributed, multi-agent systems using the **Model Context Protocol (FastMCP)** and **LangGraph**, strictly compartmentalizing physical actuation and semantic vector memory.
- **Predictive Control (MPC) & Optimization:** Developing hybrid AI models and metaheuristics (Slime Mould Algorithm, Pareto frontiers) to optimize multi-objective trade-offs between energy consumption and comfort.
- **MLOps & Industrialization:** Implementing rigorous software engineering standards (Kedro pipelines, Bayesian hyperparameter tuning, strict linting with Ruff, automated Pytest suites).

🎓 **Education:** CY Tech (Master of Engineering in Computer Science & AI) · Reykjavík University (International AI & Deep Learning semester) · Lycée Condorcet (Classes Préparatoires MP).  
💼 **Experience:** Research & AI Engineer Apprentice at **Vestaclim** (in collaboration with **ARMINES - Mines Paris**) · Cybersecurity Engineering Intern at **Emeria**.
